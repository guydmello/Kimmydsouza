ABOUT ME

Kimmy D’Souza is a South Asian & Canadian musical theatre performer, actor, singer, and dancer with a deep love for storytelling and human connection. A recent graduate of the SLC & Queen’s University Bachelor of Music Theatre program, she is passionate about continuing to grow, staying curious, and embracing every opportunity to learn through her work as an artist.

Alongside performing, Kimmy also works as a director and choreographer and is drawn to creative spaces that value collaboration, kindness, and character-driven storytelling. She believes theatre should feel joyful and meaningful, and that the best work happens when people feel safe to take risks and bring their full selves into the room. Outside of performing, Kimmy is also an arts educator at Young People’s Theatre, where she loves working with young artists and helping them build confidence, creativity, and a genuine love for the arts.

When she is not singing or dancing, Kimmy enjoys traveling, cooking, and spending time with her sweet senior dog, Tyson. She is endlessly grateful to her family, friends, and partner for their constant belief and support. Ready for whatever comes next, Kimmy looks forward to embracing new experiences with openness, joy, and a sense of adventure.
